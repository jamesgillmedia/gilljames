What I learned:
- **This explains "not enough of my best work".** v5 was built on Weird Kid, Old Man Canyon, Sleepwalker, FIAT and Mansions. Gill hardly uses those. Gill's reels are built on John Frieda, Braun, Arc'teryx, BMW Drift, AXE, Backward Fall, Verona's Obsession, Yara, Claritin and BONSAI. v6 is rebuilt around those, using the same moments Gill picked (the in-points come from the matches).
- **How Gill opens and closes.** Gill opens cold on one held, low-key, backlit image (the 2025 reel's amber baseball portrait; the older reel's Backward Fall field). The 2025 reel closes on a BMW Drift block, then Verona's Obsession snow forest, then the title. v6 opens cold on Arc'teryx window light and closes on that BMW → Verona's snow-forest run.
- **How Gill cuts.** Projects come in blocks of 3–10 shots. Cuts are fast (median 0.9 s) on action, with whips, and there are a few long holds (Yara, Verona, Arc'teryx) to breathe. The look is low-key and contrasty, with backlight, silhouettes and flares. v6 keeps that block structure and roughly that pace (0.95 s mean), with holds of 1.5–3 s on the hero frames.

## 6. Self-review (judged against a top-tier DP reel) and open weak spots

Redone during review: dark BONSAI laser shots replaced (Allan Rayman balloons; AXE rope slam for the drum stutter); John Frieda shots with on-screen text ("Every moment", "blonde Sea Waves") removed; the BMW block re-picked from a per-second audit, because v1 of the block was four near-identical grey wides; the zoom-through the red ring rebuilt as a real hole matte (the first version read like a picture-in-picture box); 26 in-points moved so no shot crosses a source edit; two accidental duplicates fixed (Stay Gold flare, AXE gym).

Still open:
- **Coca-Cola and BMW Legends are missing.** Site #2 and #3, but they're only on the box as the 360p Demo Reel capture. If Gill can provide files, they should go straight into chapter B.
- AXE Stay Gold is only the 12.7 s site loop, so it gets three short shots.
- The photo / object / space walls (≈12 s) are the v5 system with new ways in. They're the least "Gill-reel"-like part. Gill's own reels have no stills at all. They're kept because earlier rounds asked for them; trim them first if Gill wants even more film.
- Music is still the v5 re-edit of "To Go" (quiet intro, breakdown at 0:27). The drum stutter and freeze land on its fills, but the track has no big drum hits, so the stutters read gentler than on a percussive track.
- Some Gill-favourite moments are only 0.3–0.7 s long in the source (John Frieda portrait, Braun casino). They're shown slow-motion (0.45–0.8×), which softens them slightly.
- Arc'teryx opening and orange jacket go very dark by their tail. That's intentional low-key, but on a bright phone screen the first 3 s are moody rather than punchy.
