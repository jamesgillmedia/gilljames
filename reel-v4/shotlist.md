# gill james: reel 2026 v4 shot list

v4 is a rework of v3, not a patch. All source footage and stills were audited again. Each film was sampled once per shot (1,000+ frames) and scored for sharpness and exposure, and every joio.co product photo was scored the same way. Then shots were chosen by eye. Contact sheets for the audit are in /workspace/reels/work/v4/film/ and obj_*.jpg.

Music: Lost Coffee, "To Go" (same track and the same 70.7 s window as v3; draft audio from YouTube until Gill supplies the master). Tempo 97.6 BPM, so 1 beat = 0.615 s. Every cut is on the beat grid. Most cuts are on whole beats. A few product shots are 2.5 beats, so some cuts land on the half beat. The six big hits (0:01.2, 0:13.5, 0:25.8, 0:38.1, 0:50.4, 1:02.7) open the sections: film, object, film + photo, space, the climax, and the button.

Picture: film runs in a 2.39:1 matte inside the 1920x1080 frame. **Objects break out of the matte to full 16:9**, so product photos are as big as the screen allows. Products appear in one of three ways: as a whole photo at full frame height over a blurred copy of itself, as a 16:9 crop around the object, or as a same-object stack of 2 to 4 panels that all appear at once. Type is lowercase Poppins, white on black. ᑕᐣᓯ is set in Noto Sans Canadian Aboriginal.

Only Gill's real existing work is used: film footage, joio.co product photography, Gill's stills, and Gill's own spatial-design pages. No AI renders, site mood frames, signature animation files, cedar studio or pavilion content, or @Kodafy material. No upscaled 360p capture footage either.

## Remaining weak spots
- **Space is still design boards.** No photographs of a built interior or installation exist anywhere on the box, so space is the sharpest of Gill's own spatial pages: the pond/forest split, the dome-interior wireframe and the circular plan (4.3 s). The soft forest-garden boards were dropped. The pages are 1206 px phone screenshots. Like the product heroes, they now sit at full height (or full width) over a blurred copy of themselves, but they are still drawings, not photos of a built space.
- **Photo is thin (5 shots, 3.7 s).** These are stills from Gill's unlisted stills pages (snow forest, green field, tunnel light, girl in a field, pink house). Gill's B&W art photos were left out because of the B&W-crush note. The joio.co product shots are also Gill's photography, so the object section partly carries the photo discipline.
- **The product photos are phone originals (1440x1800 or 3024x4032).** Whole-photo heroes are downscaled, so they're sharp. The 16:9 crops (cup 0:15.4, moss 0:50.4, plant 0:53.2, broom tray 0:57.5) are upscaled about 1.3x with light sharpening. They hold up on a phone, but are the softest object frames at 1080p.
- **No joio.co video exists on the box.** The joio.co landing page only links to shop.joioco.com, which currently returns "store unavailable", and the Instagram widget is blocked (401). If Gill has product clips or higher-res product photos, they would drop straight into the object slots.
- **The silhouette kiss (0:02.2) is a dark, low-key shot on purpose.** It's kept because the review praised it and it is not a product. Nothing in the reel has been brightened.
- **The music is draft audio from YouTube.** Re-mux with Gill's master when it arrives. The cut timings won't change.
