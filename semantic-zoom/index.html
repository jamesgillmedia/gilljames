BTHE RULES
numbered pins on the frames
1
Each level adds information. L0 names → L1 projects + counts → L2 thumbnails with title, type, year → L3 caption + credits. Nothing is just made bigger.
2
Stable layout. Items stay where they were and gain detail; film's tile at L1 is where film's grid opens at L2. No reshuffle between levels.
3
Snaps to 4 levels. A pinch settles on L0, L1, L2 or L3, never in between. A tiny ●○○○ indicator shows the level.
4
A tap = zooming in on that item; buttons for every gesture. Tap a tile or frame to go one level in (same as pinching out on it). + and − (≥ 44 px) do what pinch out / pinch in do. No gesture is the only way.
5
Tab bar always on; every level has a real URL. / → /all/ → /film/ → /film/#play=27. Back = one level out. Links and reloads land on the same level.
6
Works with no WebGL, reduced motion, JS off. The levels become plain pages and lists: the same four URLs, plain links, no animation. Pinch falls back to normal browser zoom.
7
Tested on a real Android phone and a real iPhone before Gill sees it. Gill's Galaxy S24 plus an iPhone, portrait and landscape, by hand: pinch, tap, +/−, back. Emulators alone don't count.
CWHY THE LAST BUILD BROKE
margin note
!
Too many layered gestures on one surface: tap the water = dive, swipe = dive, pinch = fly or page zoom, drag = pan, pull down = close. Each fix added a guard for the others.
!
Hidden tap layers: invisible bars that still caught taps, and overlapping name hit-areas that opened the neighbour (C-09 H5; round 4 (d)).
!
Layouts that differ by orientation: landscape names collided and mis-tapped, the landscape reel was a 335×50 sliver, the poem was cut in landscape (round 4 HIGH 1–3).
Semantic zoom has one gesture with one meaning, no hidden layer, and the same four levels in either orientation.
