gill james

I am Gill
I am James
I am an artist
a Cree voice rising
weaving sound into the unseen
Indigenous kin, my culture’s pulse
visual arts
Fine art and music
are rivers I swim, a daily drift
a lifelong hum
Artistry a starfield I chase
All my relations,
in all my creations
their breath in every chord I make and everything
I create.
