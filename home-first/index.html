★fits best: B · Moonwater Wall
All three pass every collision check: they share the same flow rules, so nothing can overlap. The difference is fit. On the S24 portrait screen B shows the whole poem and all five chapters, 54 px to spare, because its chapters are one 78 px row. A's second row ends 4 px under the bar; C's third row 12 px under it (rows of 44 px instead of 48 would close that, still ≥ 44). In landscape A and C show every chapter and B shows 3 of 5. On the iPhone SE no concept reaches its chapters without one scroll: the poem alone ends 38 px above the bar.
i
Content that runs below the bar is not a collision: the bar is solid and the page scrolls. The check clips content at the bar's top edge.
ELAYOUT RULES THAT MAKE COLLISIONS IMPOSSIBLE
margin notes · all in home-first.css
1
One column, in flow. Header, name, roles, poem, chapters stack; each takes its own height. No hand-placed coordinates.
2
Nothing is position:absolute. The only fixed layers are the backdrop (no text, no taps, z-index −1) and the bar.
3
The poem wraps or pages, never overlaps. It pushes everything below it down; if it's long, the page scrolls.
4
The bar reserves its own space: body padding-bottom = bar height + safe-area inset, so the last item ends above it.
5
Landscape = two columns (name + poem left, chapters right) and the page scrolls. Nothing is hidden or shrunk.
6
Size with dvh and env(safe-area-inset-*), and test with the URL bar showing (the smallest viewport).
