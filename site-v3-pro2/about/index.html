Cinematographer + Artist + Spatial Designer

bio

I'm Gill James (he/him), a Cree-Métis artist with roots in Norway House Cree Nation. I live in Vancouver.

After graduating from UBC Film Production in 2014, building on a design and advertising background from OCAD, I spent more than a decade as a cinematographer and producer. My cinematography on One With the Devil won the CSC Student Cinematography Award (Panavision Canada) and the Leo Award for Best Cinematography in a Short Drama (2015), with further Leo nominations for Arc (2015) and Backward Fall (2014). As producer of Backward Fall, I also shared in its Leo Award for Best Student Production (2014).

Now retired from cinematography, I make objects, spaces, light and sound as one practice. I created Spirit Eye of skwácháýs, a public art concept recognized with Best Public Art Concept and Council's Choice within the BIDC Public Art Intensive in 2025. I'm developing a yellow cedar piece staged with light, sound and film. I make music as Mothers Moniker and Lost Coffee.

Outside the studio, I do trail stewardship. Time on the trails shapes how I think about place, material and light.
